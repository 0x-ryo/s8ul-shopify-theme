Return & Replacement Policy

At S8UL.club, we strive to provide our customers with quality jerseys and a smooth shopping experience.

Please read our Return & Replacement Policy carefully before placing an order.

1. No Return & No Replacement

All purchases made on S8UL.club are final.

We do not offer returns, exchanges, or replacements on any products purchased through our website.

Once an order has been placed and confirmed, it cannot be returned or exchanged for another size, color, design, or product.

We request that customers carefully review their product selection, size, and order details before completing their purchase.

2. Size Selection

We recommend checking the size chart available on the product page before placing your order.

Please ensure that you select the correct size according to your measurements and preferences.

S8UL.club will not be responsible for size-related issues arising from incorrect size selection by the customer.

We do not offer size exchanges or replacements once an order has been delivered.

3. Product Images & Descriptions

We make every effort to display accurate product images, colors, and descriptions on our website.

However, slight variations in color, appearance, or details may occur due to screen settings, lighting conditions, or manufacturing differences.

Such minor variations will not qualify for a return, exchange, or replacement.

4. Order Cancellation

Once an order has been confirmed, cancellation requests may not be accepted.

Orders that have already been processed, packed, or shipped cannot be cancelled.

Please review your order details carefully before making a purchase.

5. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Products

All products are checked before dispatch to ensure that they meet our quality standards.

Since S8UL.club follows a strict no-return and no-replacement policy, we do not offer returns or replacements for damaged, defective, or incorrect products unless required by applicable law.

If you receive a product that appears damaged or incorrect, please contact our customer support team with your order details and supporting photographs.

We will review your concern and respond accordingly.

6. Refunds

We do not offer refunds for purchases made on S8UL.club, except where required by applicable law or where a refund is otherwise expressly approved by us.

In cases where a refund is approved, the amount will be processed through the original payment method or another appropriate method.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment service provider.

We will make reasonable efforts to address your concerns.

By placing an order on S8UL.club, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Return & Replacement Policy.
