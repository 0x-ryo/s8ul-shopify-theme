Shipping & Delivery Policy

At S8UL.club, we aim to deliver your favorite jerseys safely and on time.

This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how we process, ship, and deliver orders placed through our website.

1. Order Processing

Once you place an order on S8UL.club, our team begins processing it.

Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation.

Order processing includes:

  • Payment verification
  • Order confirmation
  • Product preparation and packaging
  • Dispatch through our shipping partners

Order processing time may vary depending on product availability, order volume, and operational requirements.

2. Estimated Delivery Time

We aim to deliver orders within 7–14 business days, depending on the customer's city and delivery location.

Delivery timelines may vary based on:

  • Customer's city and PIN code
  • Courier partner availability
  • Shipping distance
  • Order processing time
  • Public holidays
  • Weather conditions
  • Other unforeseen circumstances

The estimated delivery timeline begins after the order has been confirmed and processed.

Please note that delivery timelines are estimates and are not guaranteed.

3. Shipping Locations

S8UL.club currently aims to deliver orders across serviceable locations in India.

Delivery availability depends on the shipping partner's serviceability for the customer's PIN code.

If your location is not serviceable, we may contact you to discuss the available options.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before you complete your purchase.

Shipping charges may vary depending on the delivery location, order value, or applicable promotional offers.

Any applicable shipping fees will be communicated clearly before order confirmation.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you may receive a shipping confirmation containing tracking details.

You can use the tracking information provided by our shipping partner to monitor your shipment.

Tracking updates may take some time to appear after dispatch.

6. Delivery Delays

While we aim to deliver orders within the estimated 7–14 business day timeframe, delays may occasionally occur due to circumstances beyond our control.

These may include:

  • Courier operational delays
  • Weather disruptions
  • Public holidays
  • High order volumes
  • Incorrect shipping information
  • Remote or restricted delivery locations

S8UL.club will make reasonable efforts to assist customers in tracking delayed shipments.

7. Incorrect Shipping Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping information at checkout.

Please ensure that your name, mobile number, shipping address, and PIN code are correct.

S8UL.club will not be responsible for delivery delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer, subject to applicable law.

If you notice an error in your shipping details, please contact us as soon as possible.

We cannot guarantee that shipping information can be changed after an order has been processed or dispatched.
