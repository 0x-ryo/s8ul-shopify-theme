Privacy Policy

At S8UL.club, your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, browse our products, or make a purchase.

By accessing or using S8UL.club, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website or make a purchase, we may collect certain information to provide you with a seamless shopping experience.

Personal Information

When you place an order, create an account, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and shipping address
  • Account login information, if you create an account
  • Order and purchase history

Order Information

When you purchase a jersey or other merchandise from S8UL.club, we may collect information related to your order, including:

  • Products purchased
  • Jersey size and quantity
  • Player name or number customization, if available
  • Order confirmation and fulfillment details
  • Shipping and delivery information

Payment Information

Payments are processed through third-party payment service providers.

We may receive transaction details such as payment status, transaction reference numbers, and payment method information.

We do not store your complete credit card details, banking credentials, or UPI PIN on our servers.

Technical Information

When you browse our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and website usage data.

This information helps us maintain and improve our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders.
  • Confirm purchases and provide order updates.
  • Arrange shipping and delivery.
  • Process returns, exchanges, and refunds.
  • Respond to customer inquiries.
  • Improve our website and shopping experience.
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions.
  • Maintain the security of our services.
  • Send promotional communications where permitted.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

We use your information only for legitimate business purposes and as described in this Privacy Policy.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our store and fulfill your orders.

These may include:

  • Payment Providers: To process payments, verify transactions, and manage refunds.
  • Shipping Partners: To deliver your orders using the name, phone number, and shipping address you provide.
  • Website Service Providers: To support website hosting, e-commerce functionality, analytics, customer support, and security.
  • Legal Authorities: When disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

We share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

4. Cookies and Website Technologies

S8UL.club may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience.

These technologies may help us:

  • Remember your shopping cart.
  • Maintain your login session.
  • Save your preferences.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Measure marketing effectiveness.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

Where required by law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies.

5. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional emails or messages about new collections, product launches, offers, and other store updates where permitted by applicable law.

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails using the unsubscribe link included in the message.

You may also contact us to request that you no longer receive marketing communications.

Please note that you may still receive essential transactional communications, such as order confirmations, shipping updates, and refund notifications.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

These measures may include secure connections, access controls, and appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill your orders.
  • Maintain purchase and transaction records.
  • Handle returns, exchanges, and disputes.
  • Prevent fraud.
  • Meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

When your information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.

Certain information may need to be retained to complete transactions, fulfill legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

To exercise your privacy rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies.

S8UL.club is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for individuals who are below the minimum legal age required to use our services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation, including deletion where required by law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website, or legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact us.
